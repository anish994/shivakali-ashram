EXTREME DANGER

🔥
Power:

Complete kundalini awakening, ego death, liberation from samsara, transcendence of duality

⚠️
Extreme Risks:

Physical death, mental breakdown, complete personality dissolution, irreversible kundalini psychosis

🛡️
Requirements:
  • • 20+ years of tantric sadhana under guru
  • • Complete mastery of all other Mahavidyas
  • • Fearlessness in facing death
  • • Temple worship ONLY - never home practice
  • • Specific cremation ground rituals

🔥 Historical accounts document multiple practitioners dying during Chinnamasta sadhana. This is NOT hyperbole.

Shamshan Kali Yantra

EXTREME DANGER
▼

⚠️ CREMATION GROUND PRACTICE ONLY

Shamshan (cremation ground) Kali is the most fierce tantric form - practiced only at midnight in cremation grounds.

The Dark Mother of Destruction

Unlike the more benevolent Dakshina Kali, Shamshan Kali is worshipped for ultimate tantric powers, complete ego destruction, and mastery over death itself. She stands on Shiva's corpse in the cremation ground.

🌑
Practice Location:

Midnight at cremation grounds during Amavasya (new moon) - NEVER at home

🩸
Offerings Required:

Blood, meat, alcohol, cremation ash - practices that break all social norms

⚡
Powers Granted:

Complete control over death, siddhis (supernatural powers), ability to resurrect the dead, mastery of tantra

⚠️ Practitioners have been found dead at cremation grounds, their bodies in meditative posture with no physical cause of death.

Bhairava Yantra

HIGH DANGER
▼

The Fearsome Guardian of Time

Bhairava is Shiva in his most fierce form - the annihilator of fear, master of time (Kaal Bhairava), and guardian of tantric knowledge. His yantra grants protection and time mastery but demands absolute fearlessness.

🐕
Sacred Symbolism:

Black dog as vahana, skull garland, trident, controls the wheel of time

⏰
Powers:

Control over time, protection from all enemies, tantric siddhis, fearlessness

⚠️
Requirements:
  • • Guru diksha (initiation) essential
  • • Midnight practice during Rahu Kaal
  • • Complete fearlessness - any fear brings disaster
  • • Strict dietary and purity observances

💡 If you experience deep fear during Bhairava sadhana, STOP IMMEDIATELY. Bhairava tests the practitioner's fearlessness.
