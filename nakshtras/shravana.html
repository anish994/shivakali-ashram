SHRAVANA

"To Hear"

The Listener • The Learner • The Connector

You are the cosmic listener of reality, the one who understands that knowledge is a sacred vibration. Where others hear noise, you discern wisdom. You don't just hear—you attune to the subtle frequencies of truth.

Modern physics confirms your essence: information is the fabric of the universe. Shravana operates the same way—by listening deeply, life becomes connected, insightful, and full of purpose.

Welcome to your divine assignment, Keeper of the Cosmic Sound. You're not here to speak—you’re here to listen. 👂

Mythological Foundation

The Ancient Wisdom Behind Cosmic Listening

👣
▼

Vishnu's Three Steps

Shravana is associated with Vishnu, who took three giant steps to traverse the universe.
Cosmic Reach: The three steps symbolize the power to encompass all of existence and to understand the interconnectedness of all things.
Divine Knowledge: This myth highlights the ability of Shravana to attain profound knowledge and to see the bigger picture.
Transcendent Power: The three steps also represent the ability to transcend the limitations of the physical world and to access higher realms of consciousness.
👂
▼

The Ear

The primary symbol of Shravana is the ear, representing the power of listening.
Receptive Listening: Shravana is not just about hearing, but about deep, receptive listening that leads to true understanding.
Learning and Wisdom: The ear symbolizes the importance of learning from others and from the universe itself.
Oral Tradition: This symbol connects Shravana to the ancient tradition of passing down knowledge through the spoken word.
🔱
▼

The Trident

The trident is another symbol associated with Shravana, representing the power to overcome the three forms of suffering.
Overcoming Suffering: The three prongs of the trident represent the power to overcome physical, mental, and spiritual suffering.
Divine Power: The trident is a weapon of the gods, symbolizing the divine power that can be accessed through deep listening and wisdom.
Integration: The trident also represents the integration of the three gunas (sattva, rajas, and tamas) and the three doshas (vata, pitta, and kapha).

The classical framework that defines Shravana's sacred role in cosmic listening
Deity: Vishnu — The preserver of the universe, who took three steps to traverse the cosmos.
Shakti (Power): Samhanana Shakti — The power to connect and to bring people together.
Sacred Symbol: The Ear — This symbol represents the power of listening and the importance of oral tradition.
Ruling Planet: Moon (Chandra) — The cosmic mother who governs our minds and emotions.
Elemental Nature: Air (Vayu) — Expressing as the medium of sound and communication.
Zodiac Context: 10°00' - 23°20' Capricorn — Where the practical and the spiritual meet.
Essential Nature: The Listener — The one who seeks knowledge and connects with the divine through deep listening.
