What are Nakshatras?

🌌

Ancient Stellar Science

Nakshatras are the 27 lunar mansions in Vedic astronomy, each representing a 13°20' segment of the zodiac. These stellar constellations have guided human consciousness for over 5,000 years.

🧬

Consciousness DNA

Each Nakshatra carries unique psychological and spiritual qualities that influence personality, behavior, and life purpose. Think of them as cosmic DNA strands of consciousness.

⚡

Modern Relevance

Today's neuroscience and quantum physics are discovering what Vedic seers knew: stellar frequencies directly impact human consciousness, creativity, and evolutionary potential.

Why Study Nakshatras?

Understanding your Nakshatra provides deep insights into your natural abilities, life challenges, spiritual path, and optimal ways to align with cosmic rhythms. It's like having a user manual for your consciousness written in the stars.
