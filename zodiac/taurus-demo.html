The Worrier

Integration Path: Wabi-Sabi Wisdom

[ANXIETY_MODULATION::REQUIRED] The antidote to perfectionism is embracing the beauty of imperfection (Wabi-Sabi). The evolved Virgo understands that flaws are signatures of authenticity. Integration Path: Sacred Surrender
