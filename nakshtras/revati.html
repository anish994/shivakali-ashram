Core Attributes

The classical framework that defines Revati's sacred role in the cosmos
Deity: Pushan — The divine shepherd who guides and protects all beings. Pushan is the nurturer of the cosmic flock, ensuring safe passage and providing nourishment for the soul's journey.
Shakti (Power): Kshiradyapani Shakti — The power to nourish and protect. This shakti provides the final nourishment for the soul's journey, ensuring a safe and peaceful transition to the next realm.
Sacred Symbol: A pair of fish — This symbol represents:
  • The flow of the soul through the cosmic ocean
  • The journey of the soul towards its final destination
  • The abundance of spiritual wealth
  • The vessel that carries the soul to the other side
Ruling Planet: Mercury (Budha) — The divine messenger who:
  • Illuminates the path with divine wisdom
  • Connects the individual soul with the cosmic mind
  • Facilitates communication between different realms
  • Guides the soul on its final journey
Elemental Nature: Water (Apas) dominates, expressing as:
  • Deep emotional and psychic sensitivity
  • Fluidity and adaptability
  • Access to the collective unconscious
  • The universal solvent, dissolving forms
Zodiac Context: The final degrees of Pisces (16°40' - 30°00'), where:
  • The soul prepares for its final liberation
  • The boundaries of the self dissolve into the infinite
  • Spiritual wealth culminates
  • The journey ends and a new one begins
Essential Nature: The Cosmic Guide who:
  • Guides with compassion and wisdom
  • Illuminates the path to the divine
  • Helps souls find their way home
  • Connects the finite with the infinite
