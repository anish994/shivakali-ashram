🟢 MINI RITUALS FOR RIGHT NOW

🌬️

3-Minute Nadi Reset

🟢 self-practice • nervous system

When your chest feels like it is buzzing and your thoughts are racing before a call, plant both feet on the floor. Breathe in gently, hold for a beat, then sigh the air out as if you are fogging a mirror. While you do this, press the space between your eyebrows with two fingers. Three minutes of this is often enough to cancel a panic spiral.

🕯️

One Candle, One Sentence

🟢 self-practice • focus & clearing

End the day by lighting a tea light near your bed or desk. Name one thing out loud you are done carrying today, or one thing you are willing to receive tomorrow. Then forget about it and wash your face, scroll, cook — normal life. The point is to stitch ritual into the fabric of ordinary time, not only crisis.

🪐

Planetary Hour Check

🟢 self-practice • better timing

Before you hit "send" on a big mail or walk into a high‑stakes meeting, quickly check any planetary‑hour app. If you can, slide the action into a Jupiter or Venus hour for support, or a Sun hour for visibility. No lamps, no mantras — just using the cosmic weather report instead of pretending it does not exist.

📿

Heart Tap Mantra

🟢 self-practice • emotional reset

When emotion spikes and your fingers want to type a paragraph of rage, park the phone. One palm on your chest, the other gently tapping the sternum. Whisper any heart mantra or simple line like "I am here". A minute of this often saves you from messages that burn bridges you actually want to keep.

🧰 PREPARATION PROTOCOL

📋

Before Any Deep Work

Basic system hygiene

Sleep, hydrate, eat something grounding. Avoid heavy intoxication for at least 24 hours. If your body is already overloaded, we start with stabilization before any big ritual engines.

🚪

Space Setup

Door closed, phone down

For self‑practice, choose a small, contained space. Switch your phone to silent, tell people you will be unavailable for a short window, and have water + a blanket nearby so landing is easy.

🧠

Mental Health Boundary

Ritual ≠ replacement for care

If you are in acute psychiatric crisis or actively suicidal, contact local emergency / professional support first. Ritual can support healing, but it is not a substitute for medical or psychiatric treatment.

⛔

Not a Fit If...

Wrong reasons, wrong timing

This path is not for entertainment, revenge or proving you are powerful. If you only want to harm others, bypass your own responsibility, or treat this like a magic show, ShivaKali Ashram is not the right temple for you.
