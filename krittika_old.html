Mythological Foundation

🔥 The Seven Sacred Flames

In Vedic lore, Krittika represents the seven sacred flames that nurse and protect baby Kartikeya (the god of war and wisdom). These aren't ordinary fires — they are the primordial flames of purification that existed before creation itself. Each flame carries a different aspect of divine fire: the flame that burns ignorance, the flame that forges character, the flame that illuminates truth, the flame that protects innocence, the flame that transforms, the flame that judges, and the flame that creates. Together, they form the cosmic forge where souls are refined from their crude state into luminous beings of wisdom and power.

The Divine Nurses

The seven stars of Krittika served as celestial nurses to baby Kartikeya, representing the nurturing aspect of purifying fire.

  • Fierce love that demands growth and excellence
  • Creating strength through controlled challenges
