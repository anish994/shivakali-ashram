## 🎯 TOOL IMPLEMENTATION EXAMPLES

### **Mars Strength Analyzer - Detailed Concept:**

#### **Assessment Questions (5-7 questions):**
1. **Conflict Response:** "When facing obstacles, do you: A) Push through directly B) Find creative solutions C) Seek help D) Avoid confrontation"
2. **Energy Pattern:** "Your energy levels are: A) Consistently high B) Bursts of intensity C) Steady moderate D) Variable"
3. **Competition Style:** "In competition, you: A) Must win at all costs B) Enjoy the challenge C) Prefer collaboration D) Avoid competition"
4. **Decision Making:** "When making quick decisions, you: A) Trust your gut immediately B) Consider all options C) Seek advice D) Postpone when possible"
5. **Physical Activity:** "Your relationship with exercise: A) Love intense workouts B) Prefer moderate activity C) Enjoy group activities D) Avoid physical exertion"

#### **Results Processing:**
- **Scoring Algorithm:** Weight responses to determine Mars strength (1-100)
- **Archetype Assignment:** Strategic Warrior, Passionate Creator, Protective Guardian, Diplomatic Peacemaker
- **Strength Assessment:** Excessive Mars, Balanced Mars, Weak Mars, Variable Mars

#### **Personalized Recommendations:**
- **For Strong Mars:** Cooling practices, leadership roles, competitive outlets
- **For Weak Mars:** Energy-building activities, assertiveness training, confidence exercises
- **For Excessive Mars:** Diplomatic skills, patience practices, cooling techniques
- **Universal:** Tuesday rituals, martial arts, spicy foods, red coral gemstone

#### **Results Presentation:**
- **Visual Meter:** Mars strength gauge with color coding
- **Archetype Card:** Detailed description with strengths/challenges
- **Action Plan:** 3-5 specific recommendations
- **Cross-References:** Links to relevant Mars knowledge content
- **Progress Tracking:** Compare results over time with retakes
